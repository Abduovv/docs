The Arcadia leaderboard gives you a ranked, data-driven view of every verified trader on the platform. Before you allocate a single dollar, spend time here — not just glancing at recent returns, but examining the full picture: score confidence, drawdown history, vault capacity, and classification. Every metric you see is derived from on-chain activity and cannot be manually edited or inflated.

## The Leaderboard

The leaderboard offers two default sort views, each surfacing a different dimension of trader quality:

- **Top 100 by Arcadia Score** — ranks traders by their overall risk-adjusted reputation score (0–1000). This view rewards consistency, discipline, and longevity over short-term luck.
- **Top 100 by 30-Day Return** — ranks traders by recent performance. Useful for spotting momentum, but always cross-reference with drawdown and confidence before acting on it.

You can filter and sort by tier, confidence level, style tags, and capacity availability. Use these filters to narrow the field before diving into individual profiles.

## Key Metrics Explained

Every trader card and profile exposes the same core metrics. Use this table as a reference when evaluating who to back:

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| Arcadia Score | Overall risk-adjusted reputation (0–1000) |
| Tier | Score bracket and capacity multiplier (Verified → Established → Advanced → Elite → Apex) |
| Confidence | How much trading history backs the score (low / med / high) |
| 30-Day Return | Recent performance over the last 30 days |
| Max Drawdown | Worst peak-to-trough loss recorded on-chain |
| Sortino Ratio | Downside-only risk-adjusted return — penalizes harmful volatility, not upside swings |
| Capacity Used | What percentage of this vault's total allocation limit is already filled |

Higher scores mean more proven skill, but always pair the score with its confidence level. A metric like Sortino ratio matters more at high confidence than at low confidence, because the sample size is large enough to be statistically meaningful.

## Red Flags to Watch For

Not every high-ranked trader is worth backing right now. Before depositing, check for these warning signs:

- **Low confidence** — The score is based on too few trades to be reliable. A score of 912 backed by 20 trades carries far more uncertainty than an 800 score backed by 500+ trades.
- **High max drawdown** — Any max drawdown above 20% signals the trader has taken (or accepted) significant losses in the past. That level of loss in your position would be difficult to recover from.
- **`deposits_open = false`** — The vault is not currently accepting new investors. Attempting to deposit will fail. Check the vault status section on the profile page.
- **Capacity near 100%** — Very little room remains for new capital. Even if deposits are technically open, the vault may be close to its allocation ceiling, which can affect future share availability.

<Tip>
  Filter for Confidence = **high** before evaluating scores. A 912 score with only 20 trades is far less reliable than an 800 score with 500+ trades — the latter has been tested across hundreds of market conditions.
</Tip>

<Note>
  All metrics shown on Arcadia are derived from on-chain data. No trader can manually edit their score, return figures, or trade history. What you see is the verified, immutable record.
</Note>
