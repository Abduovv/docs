The prices endpoint serves Arcadia's real-time market price feed, refreshed on every slot. Each entry covers a single tradeable instrument and includes the current mid-price, 24-hour change in absolute and percentage terms, intraday high and low, and 24-hour volume. You can use this endpoint to power price tickers, display market context alongside vault trade history, or pre-validate entry prices before submitting orders.

## Request

This endpoint requires no parameters and no authentication.

```bash
curl -X GET "https://api.arcadia.trade/v1/prices"
```

---

## Response

The response is an array of `PriceData` objects — one per supported market. The array order is not guaranteed; use the `market` field as the stable identifier.

<ResponseField name="market" type="string">
  The market symbol in quote-slash-base format, e.g. `"XAU/USD"`,
  `"EUR/USD"`, `"BTC/USD"`. This string matches the `market` field in trade
  history records.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="price" type="number">
  The current mid-price for this market at the time of the last feed refresh.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="change_24h" type="number">
  Absolute price change over the trailing 24 hours (current price minus the
  price 24 hours ago). Negative values indicate a price decline.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="change_pct_24h" type="number">
  Percentage price change over the trailing 24 hours (e.g. `1.23` = +1.23%,
  `-0.45` = -0.45%).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="volume_24h" type="number">
  Aggregate notional trading volume across all Arcadia vaults for this market
  over the trailing 24 hours, in USDC.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="high_24h" type="number">
  The highest price recorded for this market in the trailing 24-hour window.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="low_24h" type="number">
  The lowest price recorded for this market in the trailing 24-hour window.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ts" type="number">
  Unix timestamp (seconds) of the most recent price update reflected in this
  entry.
</ResponseField>
