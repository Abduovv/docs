The Arcadia Score is a single number between 0 and 1000 that captures how well a trader manages risk and generates returns over time. It is computed entirely from on-chain trade data: no self-reporting, no manual verification, no room for manipulation. Investors use the score to evaluate traders before allocating capital, and the protocol uses it to determine which tier a trader belongs to, controlling how much external capital they can accept and what profit share they earn. A higher score means more capital access and better economics, which means the score creates a direct, ongoing incentive for disciplined trading.

## What the Score Reflects

The Arcadia Score blends three ideas into a single number:

- **Quality of trading.** A composite of risk-adjusted performance metrics computed from your on-chain history. Strong risk-adjusted returns push this up; volatile or lossy trading pulls it down.
- **Confidence in the signal.** How much history the score is based on. A brand-new trader with a handful of trades cannot yet have proven anything statistically, so their score is intentionally discounted until a real track record accumulates. Confidence grows as your on-chain trade count deepens.
- **Risk guardrails.** A safety check on catastrophic behavior. Excessive liquidations or deep peak-to-trough losses reduce your score regardless of how strong your other metrics look. The guard exists so a single blowup or a pattern of over-leveraged positions cannot be papered over by good averages.

All return calculations use **Time-Weighted Return (TWR)**, which strips out the effect of deposits and withdrawals so that your score reflects trading skill rather than capital flow timing.

## Metrics Used

The quality component draws from a set of standard risk-adjusted performance metrics computed over your on-chain history:

- **Sharpe ratio** — return relative to total volatility.
- **Sortino ratio** — return relative to downside volatility only, so upside swings are not penalized.
- **Maximum drawdown** — worst peak-to-trough loss over the vault's history.
- **Calmar ratio** — return relative to maximum drawdown.
- **Return volatility** — how consistent your returns are over time.
- **Downside deviation** — magnitude of returns that fall below a target threshold.
- **Win rate** — share of closed trades that were profitable.
- **Trade depth** — total number of closed trades on-chain, which feeds the confidence component.

Risk-adjusted metrics carry more weight than raw return, so posting a few large wins while taking excessive risk does not produce a high score. Consistency and downside control dominate the signal.

## Confidence: Why New Traders Start Conservative

A small sample of trades can produce impressive-looking metrics by chance. Ten trades with a great Sharpe ratio prove very little. To protect investors, Arcadia discounts the score of traders with short histories and lets it rise as their on-chain trade count grows. There is no shortcut: you earn confidence by trading. A long, verifiable track record is worth more than a short, lucky one.

## Risk Guardrails

The score includes a safety check for catastrophic risk profiles. Two behaviors reduce the score even when the underlying performance metrics look strong:

- **Frequent liquidations.** A pattern of positions ending in liquidation signals over-leverage or under-sized risk buffers, and drags the score down.
- **Deep drawdowns.** Large peak-to-trough losses damage investor capital and indicate insufficient downside protection. Sustained or severe drawdowns reduce the score.

The exact thresholds are set by the protocol and can be tuned over time. The principle is stable: catastrophic risk is penalized, not averaged away.

## Score Range Reference

| Score | What It Signals |
| --- | --- |
| 100–249 | New or early-stage trader; limited track record, entering the Verified tier |
| 250–499 | Established track record with reasonable risk management; Established tier |
| 500–749 | Consistent risk-adjusted performance across meaningful sample size; Advanced tier |
| 750–949 | Strong long-run metrics with low drawdown and high confidence; Elite tier |
| 950–1000 | Exceptional performance across every dimension; Apex tier |

New traders receive a score floor of 100 — you enter the protocol at the Verified tier rather than starting from zero. The ceiling of 1000 is a hard cap; no score can exceed it.

<Note>
  The Arcadia Score updates hourly and is computed entirely from on-chain trade data. Because every trade, position, and liquidation event is recorded on-chain, the score cannot be manipulated through off-chain reporting or manual overrides.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Consistent risk management matters more than any single large win. Steady, controlled returns outscore high-variance trading over time, and that score holds up as your confidence grows with more trades on-chain.
</Tip>
