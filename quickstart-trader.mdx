Your trading edge deserves capital to match it. Arcadia turns your on-chain history into a verified Arcadia Score — a tamper-proof, continuously updated reputation number that investors use to allocate real USDC into your vault. This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a fresh wallet connection to an active, investor-ready trader profile on Solana.

<Note>
  Arcadia is currently in **private beta**. You will need a waitlist invitation to create a trader profile. [Join the waitlist →](https://arcadia.trade)
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Arcadia uses **Sign-In With Solana (SIWS)** for authentication — no passwords, no email, no centralized accounts. Your wallet signature is your identity.

    1. Navigate to [app.arcadia.trade](https://app.arcadia.trade) and click **Connect Wallet**.
    2. Select **Phantom** or **Solflare** from the wallet picker.
    3. Approve the connection request in your wallet extension.
    4. Sign the SIWS authentication message when prompted — this is a free, off-chain signature that proves wallet ownership and issues you a session JWT.

    <Tip>
      The SIWS message includes a timestamp and domain. Never sign a SIWS message from a domain other than `app.arcadia.trade` — it cannot be replayed, but phishing sites may attempt to impersonate the app.
    </Tip>

    Once signed in, your wallet address becomes your persistent trader identity on Arcadia.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initialize your trader profile">
    Before any investor capital can enter your vault, you need to create your on-chain trader profile and seed your vault with your own funds.

    1. From your dashboard, click **Initialize Vault**.
    2. Review the vault creation transaction — this deploys your personal vault program account on Solana.
    3. Approve the transaction in your wallet. A small SOL amount is required for account rent.
    4. **Self-fund your vault** by depositing USDC into your own vault. This is a hard requirement before investor deposits are permitted.

    <Note>
      You must self-fund your vault before investors can allocate capital to you. This requirement aligns your interests with your investors — you have real skin in the game before they do.
    </Note>

    Your vault starts with deposits closed. You control when you open your vault to investors after you've established a score.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start trading">
    With your vault funded, you're ready to trade. Open the **Trading Terminal** from your dashboard to access all supported markets.

    **Available markets:**
    - Forex: XAU/USD, XAG/USD, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY
    - Solana perpetual DEXs (supported venues listed in the terminal)

    Every trade you execute — entry price, size, duration, exit, PnL — is recorded on-chain in real time. There is no off-chain shadow book. Your entire history is public, auditable, and feeds directly into your Arcadia Score calculation.

    <Tip>
      Consistency matters more than single large wins. The Arcadia Score weights risk-adjusted metrics like Sharpe and Sortino ratio heavily, so a steady, low-drawdown track record will outperform a volatile one over time.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build your score and unlock investor capital">
    Your Arcadia Score updates continuously as you trade. As your score crosses tier thresholds, your vault's investor capacity increases and your profit share rate rises.

    Once you're satisfied with your score, open your vault to investors from the **Vault Settings** panel by enabling deposits. Investors browsing the leaderboard will now be able to allocate USDC to your vault.

    Your profit share is automatically calculated on-chain at settlement — no manual claims or off-chain agreements required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trader Tiers

Your Arcadia Score determines your tier, which in turn sets your **profit share rate** on investor returns and the maximum **investor capital** your vault can accept.

| Score | Tier | Profit Share |
|-------|------|:------------:|
| 100+ | Verified | 20% |
| 250+ | Established | 25% |
| 500+ | Advanced | 30% |
| 750+ | Elite | 35% |
| 950+ | Apex | 40% |

Scores below 100 are untiered. Your vault can still be self-funded and traded at any score, but investor deposits require a minimum Verified score (100+).

<Tip>
  Your tier is evaluated continuously — you can move up (or down) as your on-chain performance evolves. Focus on consistent, risk-adjusted returns to maintain and grow your tier.
</Tip>
