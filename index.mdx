Arcadia is a proof-of-performance capital protocol on Solana that bridges the gap between skilled traders and the capital they deserve. Traders build a verified Arcadia Score from their on-chain history, unlocking access to investor capital — while investors allocate USDC through non-custodial smart-contract vaults that protect their funds at every step. Whether you trade or invest, Arcadia replaces trust with transparency.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build or verify your on-chain history">
    Every trade you make on Arcadia-supported markets is recorded on Solana. The protocol continuously computes your Arcadia Score (0–1000) from time-weighted returns, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn a tier and unlock capacity">
    Cross a score threshold — Verified at 100, Established at 250, all the way to Apex at 950 — and your vault opens up to investor capital. Higher tiers unlock greater capacity and a larger profit share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investors allocate through smart-contract vaults">
    Investors deposit USDC directly into on-chain vaults. The trader controls trading strategy but can never withdraw investor funds — the smart contract enforces that boundary at all times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Performance is settled on-chain">
    Profits are distributed automatically according to your tier's profit-share rate. Every settlement is transparent, auditable, and non-custodial from deposit to withdrawal.
  </Step>
</Steps>
